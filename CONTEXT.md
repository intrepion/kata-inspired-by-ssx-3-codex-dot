# Halcyon Mountain
An original arcade snowboarding challenge circuit.

## Language
**Run**: One descent in a chosen challenge.
**Lip**: A marked ramp that launches a rider crossing its lane.
**Clean landing**: A touchdown after all trick motion has settled.
**Chain**: Consecutive clean trick jumps that increase the next landing multiplier.
**Boost**: Earned momentum reserve consumed for extra downhill speed.
**Wipeout**: A failed landing or obstacle strike that breaks the chain and slows the rider.
**Medal**: A challenge completion meeting its time and score targets.

## Mountain sample
**Heading**: The direction a rider points across or along the mountain.
**Crouch**: A compressed stance held before releasing a jump.
**Prewind**: Directional preparation that increases airborne rotation speed.
**Press**: A nose or tail balance used on snow to connect a combo.
**Rail**: An elevated line reached by jumping onto it and holding balance.
**Combo bank**: Points secured after a two-second safe interval without a trick.
**Adrenaline**: Boost reserve earned by banking tricks.
**ÜBER**: A prolonged advanced grab available at full adrenaline.
